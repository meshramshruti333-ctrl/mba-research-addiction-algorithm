# MBA RESEARCH PROJECT PROPOSAL

## **PROJECT TITLE**
**"The Addiction Algorithm: How Social Media Deliberately Makes You Scroll More"**
*A Study on Psychological Mechanisms, Business Ethics, and User Engagement Optimization*

---

## **1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**

This research explores the psychological and technological mechanisms behind social media addiction, examining how platform algorithms exploit cognitive biases and neurological responses to maximize user engagement. The study investigates the intersection of **psychology, technology, and business ethics**, providing actionable insights for marketing professionals, product managers, and ethical brand strategists.

**Target Duration**: 12-16 weeks  
**Research Approach**: Mixed-method (Quantitative + Qualitative)  
**Primary Audience**: Marketing, Product Strategy, and Digital Business professionals

---

## **2. RESEARCH OBJECTIVES**

### **Primary Objectives:**
1. Understand the psychological mechanisms (dopamine, FOMO, variable rewards) that drive addiction to social media
2. Analyze how algorithms are intentionally designed to maximize engagement and dwell time
3. Evaluate the business impact of addiction-driven design on user metrics and revenue
4. Assess ethical implications and regulatory responses to addictive design

### **Secondary Objectives:**
1. Compare addiction mechanisms across platforms (TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube)
2. Quantify the correlation between addictive features and user engagement metrics
3. Develop recommendations for ethical alternatives to addictive design
4. Propose frameworks for responsible marketing in the digital age

---

## **3. RESEARCH QUESTIONS**

**Main Research Question:**
- *How do social media algorithms exploit psychological vulnerabilities to create addictive user engagement, and what are the ethical and business implications?*

**Sub-Research Questions:**
1. What psychological triggers (dopamine, variable rewards, social validation) do algorithms exploit?
2. How do infinite scroll, notifications, and recommendation engines drive continuous engagement?
3. What is the measured impact of addictive features on user retention and business metrics?
4. How do users perceive and react to these addictive mechanisms?
5. What regulatory frameworks exist or should exist to curb addictive design?
6. How can brands market ethically while still achieving engagement goals?

## **6. RESEARCH METHODOLOGY**

### **Phase 1: Quantitative Research (Weeks 1-5)**
**Objective:** Measure engagement patterns and addiction indicators

**Methods:**
- **User Behavior Analysis**: Analyze aggregate data from social media platforms
  - Dwell time, session frequency, scroll depth, notification click-through rates
  - Compare baseline engagement vs. engagement with addictive features enabled/disabled
  
- **Survey Research** (Sample: 300-500 MBA students/young professionals)
  - Validated addiction scales (Bergen Social Media Addiction Scale)
  - Self-reported usage patterns and perceived manipulation
  - Demographic breakdowns (age, platform preference, industry)

- **Data Collection Tools**:
  - Online survey platform (Google Forms, Qualtrics)
  - Social media API data (where available)
  - Published platform metrics and research reports

### **Phase 2: Qualitative Research (Weeks 6-10)**
**Objective:** Understand user perceptions and psychological mechanisms

**Methods:**
- **In-Depth Interviews** (15-20 heavy social media users)
  - Semi-structured interviews exploring:
    - Triggers for opening apps
    - Perceived vs. actual time spent
    - Awareness of manipulation tactics
    - Emotional responses to addictive features

- **Focus Group Discussions** (3-4 groups, 8-10 participants each)
  - Discussions around ethical vs. addictive design
  - Reactions to case studies of platform algorithms
  - Preferences for alternative engagement models

- **Content Analysis**:
  - Platform feature evolution (2018-2026)
  - Marketing language used by platforms
  - Regulatory responses and policy documents

### **Phase 3: Case Study Analysis (Weeks 11-13)**
**Objective:** Deep-dive into platform-specific strategies

**Platforms to Study:**
1. **TikTok**: Infinite scroll, For You Page algorithm, short-form video addiction
2. **Instagram**: Stories, Reels, engagement metrics, notification strategy
3. **YouTube**: Recommendation algorithm, autoplay, watch time optimization
4. **LinkedIn**: Professional FOMO, engagement notifications, content virality

**Framework for Each Case:**
- Feature design rationale
- User engagement metrics
- Psychological mechanisms exploited
- Ethical criticisms and controversies

### **Phase 4: Analysis & Recommendations (Weeks 14-16)**
- Synthesize findings across all methods
- Develop ethical marketing frameworks
- Create recommendations for platforms and brands
- Prepare final presentation and report
